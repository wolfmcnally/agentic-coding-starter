## 2026-11-10 — METHODOLOGY SCOPE

Authorized outcome: Starter 1.2.0, the second of the three minor releases the operator approved in plan mode on 2026-11-10: the full gate stops re-running tests that already passed on unchanged inputs, without weakening what a push certificate binds. The operator's ruling stands that the full gate before every push stays, that its test lane may be satisfied by stored passes whose inputs are unchanged, and that a bare `./bin/test` remains an uncached full run. Tag authority for `v1.2.0` was granted with the plan.

Mechanism, stated before implementation because it refines the approved plan's wording: the plan described one stored pass per proof family keyed to that family's inputs. This change instead stores one record of the last tree on which every family is known to pass, with its per-path manifest and environment fingerprint, and at the next gate runs the families that the existing changed-path selection maps to the paths that differ from that record. The outcome the operator approved is the same; the selection rules stay in one place and remain the ones already trusted for the local-commit gate; and anything that selection cannot prove safe still widens to the full suite.

Surfaces: `lib/agentic_starter/test_governance.py` (reuse record, selection against it, a `command` proof kind for a deliverable's own test runner, treatment of bookkeeping-class paths as data), `bin/test`, `bin/check`, `bin/test-governance`, `tests/proof-estate.yaml` and its ledger, the governing sections of `policies/build-gates.md` and `policies/test-suite-governance.md`, the language-profile guidance for parallel execution in the policy and the `stamp`, `teach` and `learn` skills including a Swift profile, catalogs, `RELEASES.md`, behavioral tests and immutable records; then a signed commit, signed annotated tag, delivery through the existing remote and a hosted release entry. Reducing the cost of the slowest tests is in scope where it does not change what they prove.

Exclusions: single-record methodology closes, one-command proof admission and dashboard regeneration (1.3.0); every recipient repository; remotes, visibility, credentials and history. No recipient is inferred upgraded. In committed files the recipient whose input-keyed reuse informed this design is "the donor".
