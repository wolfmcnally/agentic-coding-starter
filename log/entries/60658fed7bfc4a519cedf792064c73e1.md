## 2026-11-20 01:15 — END — METHODOLOGY — Turn caps removed: no invocation carries a turn quota

Scope: the operator's direction of 2026-11-19, relayed by the coordinator: "excise *everything* about turn caps from the entire methodology. It's outlived its usefulness and bit us way more than it's helped." One bounded change. No replacement limit, budget or telemetry. No release in this record.

Changed:
- Configuration. `claude_max_turns` is gone from `kickoff.yaml`, the test seed, the shipped defaults, the validator and `show timeouts`. The validator refuses the key as unknown, so a project that still carries it must delete those four lines when it adopts this change.
- Launcher. `kickoff-config` no longer passes `--max-turns` to the Claude CLI, in production commands or in the sign-in and toolchain probes. The probes were capped at three and four turns; their 120-second deadline bounds them.
- Cap-hit continuation. The assessment validator, the `watch --cap-hit-assessment` option, the two diagnostic fields that recorded the cap and its receipt, and the `rescue` registration reason are removed with their documentation in the timeout policy, the kickoff and teach skills, the role and model policies, three briefs, the tools catalog and the manual.
- Proofs. The two-case cap-hit proof is deleted and retired in the ledger with a scenario-preservation assessment: every part of its scenario concerned only the removed path, and the configuration-refusal exit status its recorded defect planted stays guarded by another kickoff-config proof. Two other proofs lose only the assertions that named the cap.

Kept on purpose: the first-event, idle and hard clocks with their floors and recalibration; the limits on review passes and loop cycles, including the ten-cycle backstop; research budgets; the self-resume count; the ordinary resume-a-session option; and the watcher's turn count in its local diagnostic record, which limits nothing.

Not rewritten: log entries, archived lessons, governance ledgers and earlier release notes that mention the cap.

Verification: the kickoff-config test file, the changed-path selection and one full gate on the final tree. Remaining: a release, which waits for the operator's approval of the tag.
