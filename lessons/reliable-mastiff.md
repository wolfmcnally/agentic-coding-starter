The 2026-09-07 change settled who performs methodology work and removed independent review of it. In the same edit it removed something of a different kind: the operator's own approval of a transfer plan before anything is written. The two were treated as one ceremony, and "no repeated approval of already-authorized scope" was read as covering a gate that was the only approval there was. Invoking a skill authorizes the assessment; it had come to be read as authorizing the result.

Nothing caught it for a month. The public README, the methodology brief's skill summary and the pattern map all kept describing both skills as waiting for approval, so the corpus contradicted itself without any check failing. A test pinned the new wording, which made the removal look deliberate and would have failed a restoration.

What should be done differently: a change that removes or weakens a point where a human decides is named as its own item in the scope record and ratified by itself, separately from the routing or review change it travels with. Removing independent review and removing operator approval are different decisions. When a human gate is removed, the same change searches the outward descriptions of that surface for statements the removal makes false.
