Before scaling a model comparison, use its first predeclared cell to witness the complete native launch, review and delivery lifecycle. Fixture correctness and a green host suite prove different properties. An interactive harness's completion callback cannot be assumed to survive a print CLI exit; in the observed run, the evaluator's container lifetime followed that CLI and interrupted an unfinished role.

The operator selected repair followed by a separately identified comparison. The correction disables native background tasks for print invocations and requires an actual complete workflow witness before scaling. The corrected pair verified role and parent-process lifetime through its deadline; complete delivery remains unproved. Preserve the interrupted attempt as evaluator-affected evidence and distinguish correct code, workflow completion and model identity qualification; do not turn the interruption into a model failure or erase it from the record.

The corrected qualification witnessed complete role launches and native parent lifetime through the deadline in both harnesses, but neither delivered within its fixed budget. The renderer emitted an identifier shape that contradicted the close instruction. Correction must reach every producer of the offending output, and the proof must pass real generated output through its downstream guard. This observation supports the qualification gate; it does not establish a model ranking or justify silently extending a frozen comparison deadline.
