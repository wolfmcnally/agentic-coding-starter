A lesson filed at the moment of discovery records the *best available account* of
a mechanism — often written before the measurement discipline it prescribes
existed. That account then outlives its uncertainty: quoted in dispatches, cited
in phase specs, briefed to planners. Each hop launders a hypothesis into a
premise.

Twice in one window, a donor lesson's mechanism claim survived into downstream
work while being wrong — and in the more consequential case the error **inverted
the instrument**: the check the lesson prescribed would have *reassured* on the
exact pathology the lesson was written about.

**The rule.** Before a lesson's mechanism claim drives a design — a phase spec, a
guard, an instrument, a graduation — re-run its arithmetic against the real
artifact it describes (the trace, the index, the candidate id), not against the
lesson's prose. Both catches came the same way: not from re-reading, from
recomputing.

**Corollary for authors.** A mechanism stated without a measurement behind it
should say so in the entry. The reader least able to detect the difference is the
one designing from it.

**Corollary for graduation.** What graduates is the *corrected* mechanism. A
refutation found before graduation is cheap; the same refutation found afterward
is a wrong rule carrying the ledger's authority. That is the strongest argument
for running this check at graduation time at the latest — which in this repo is
the human's ratification step under
[`policies/lessons.md`](../policies/lessons.md), and belongs in the `sweep` pass
that proposes graduations.
