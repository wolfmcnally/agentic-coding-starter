The policy required a proof to be seen failing against a deliberate defect before it was admitted, and the ledger required a `red_witness` for every admission. Validation checked that the field was present and nonempty. An agent that wrote "removing the behavior makes this proof fail" satisfied the rule whether or not anything had been removed, and the rule read as enforced because a gate referenced the field.

The proxy was the presence of text in the field; the property was that a failure had been observed for the stated reason. A sentence that is true and a sentence that is hopeful are identical to a shape check, so the check could not tell an estate whose proofs had all been challenged from one whose proofs had only ever passed. The sibling field made the gap visible once someone counted: a per-family list of planted defects that validation accepted empty, and that was empty for nine families in ten.

**Where a rule demands that something was observed, have the tool that could observe it write the record.** A command that runs the check green, lets the defect be planted, requires the named failure, restores the tree and appends the receipt leaves nothing for a sentence to assert. Where no tool can make the observation, record the field as a claim and say so, so a later reader does not count it as evidence.

Correction applied on the operator's ruling of 2026-10-06: `./bin/test-governance witness` observes and records each red witness, and the count of proofs with no receipt is reported rather than hidden. The same pass retired the committed patch corpus, three of whose twelve held-out patches had stopped applying within a week of their last measurement.
