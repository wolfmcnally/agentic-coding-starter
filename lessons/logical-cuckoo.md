Preparing current bytes is insufficient when launch arguments can still reference an older artifact. Preserve that review as observations about its actual inputs; do not silently relabel it as a review of the final candidate. The correction used one authoritative manifest path to derive the prompt, diff, output and receipt arguments, then obtained a bounded same-reviewer verification of the complete current source and necessary adoption closure. Check the exact launch paths and input hashes together before dispatch, and verify them again at return. This is a candidate-binding lesson, not a reason to repeat successful review sequences without a changed cause.
