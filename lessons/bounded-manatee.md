Before dispatching a large implementation packet, estimate its independent
contract surfaces against the target venue's hard interaction budget. Split an
oversized packet at a coherent implementation seam while preserving the full
approved plan and complete critic review over the resulting candidate. A hard
turn ceiling is a sizing constraint, not evidence that the implementation
itself failed.
