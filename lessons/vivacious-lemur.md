Adding an atomic methodology bundle is incomplete until every propagation path
describes the same members. An executable transfer can be current while a
manual bootstrap brief, acceptance checklist, skill count, role policy, or
methodology narrative remains stale.

Inventory executable transfer skills, manual bootstrap instructions, catalogs,
acceptance lists, role output contracts, and related briefs as one propagation
boundary. The introducing change and its sweep must reconcile all of them.
