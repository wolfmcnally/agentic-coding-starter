A planner revision received the finding ledger and causal packet but not the
plan being revised. The plan remained readable, yet the role could not locate
it and regenerated the document from upstream authorities.

The reconstruction fixed the named findings and regressed everything the
reviewer was not looking at: scoped exclusions, preservation instructions, and
structured decision tables disappeared into weaker prose.

A revision packet is a diff contract, not a substitute for the artifact. It
deliberately omits unchanged content.

**The rule candidate:** every dispatch asking a role to revise prior output
supplies that exact output by path or inline and says to edit it rather than
regenerate it. Before adopting the revision, compare its load-bearing sections
and inventories with the prior artifact, not only with the findings.
