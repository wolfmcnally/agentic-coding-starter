A review brief explained evidence-ledger mechanics that the reviewer did not
operate. The reviewer had already completed its substantive work, then searched
for the implied state across temporary and home-directory trees. The search
exhausted the run and the verdict was lost.

A later attempt proved that location alone is insufficient: it remained inside
the repository but still read too much material to finish.

Two rules emerge:

- Give a delegated role the exact data it needs without describing machinery
  it cannot operate. Mechanism descriptions become invitations to investigate.
- State both where the role may read and how much it owns. Read-only access
  prevents mutation; it does not prevent wandering or context exhaustion.

A usable boundary names the repository surface, forbids unrelated state and
unrestricted recursive search, and assigns a review slice small enough to hold.
