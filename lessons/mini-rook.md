  ref: a recipient during a batch transfer — its product tests need an explicitly selected trusted executable supplied through the environment; four passing gate receipts recorded only the language runtime, so nothing said which executable earlier gates had used and the transfer stopped until the operator named one
---

When a gate's result depends on a selection made outside the tree, such as an external executable chosen through the environment, the receipt should record that selection and its identity. Otherwise a later session cannot reproduce the gate or tell that it ran under different conditions.
